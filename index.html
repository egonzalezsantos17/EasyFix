Questions people ask first

Where do you work?

We serve Cherokee County, Georgia. Call or email us and we'll work out the easiest way to get your device fixed.

How long does a repair take?

Most phone screens and batteries are done the same day. Laptops and consoles usually take one to three days, depending on parts.

Will I lose my data?

Screen, battery and port repairs don't touch your data. If a repair carries any risk to it, we tell you before we start and suggest a backup.

What if you can't fix it?

You don't pay for the diagnosis, and we'll tell you honestly whether replacing the device makes more sense.

Do you use original parts?

We tell you which part we're using before you approve the quote, so you always know what's going into your device.
